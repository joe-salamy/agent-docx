Federal Rules of Appellate Procedure, Rule 32 (effective December 1, 2024)

Rule 32(a)(4): A brief must be produced on paper that is 8 1/2 by 11 inches. The text must be double-spaced, except for quoted matter, headings, and footnotes. The margins must be at least one inch on all four sides.

Rule 32(a)(5): A proportionally spaced face must include serifs and be 14-point or larger. A monospaced face may not contain more than 10 1/2 characters per inch.

Rule 32(a)(7): A principal brief is acceptable if it contains no more than 30 pages, 13,000 words, or 1,300 lines of monospaced type. A reply brief is acceptable if it contains no more than 15 pages, 6,500 words, or 650 lines of monospaced type.

Rule 32(f): The page and word limitations do not include the corporate disclosure statement; table of contents; table of citations; statement regarding oral argument; any addendum containing statutes, rules, or regulations; certificates of counsel; signature block; proof of service; or any other item excluded by local rule.

Rule 32(g): A brief submitted under a word or line limit must include a certificate of compliance.
