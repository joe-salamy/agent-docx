United States District Court, Northern District of California Civil Local Rules (effective May 1, 2026)

Civil L.R. 3-4(a): Papers presented for filing must include counsel identification, court and division, case number, case title, and the title of the document.

Civil L.R. 3-4(c)(2): Except for the identification of counsel, title of the case, footnotes, and quotations, all text must be double-spaced, at least 12-point type, and no more than 28 lines per page. The font must be a standard, proportionally spaced serif typeface.

Civil L.R. 3-4(c)(3): Each page must contain the title of the case and case number in the footer.

Civil L.R. 7-2(b): Unless otherwise ordered, a motion must not exceed 25 pages.

Civil L.R. 7-3(a), 7-3(c), and 7-4(b): An opposition brief must not exceed 25 pages and a reply brief must not exceed 15 pages.
